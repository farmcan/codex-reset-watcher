# Evidence contract

## Source tiers

- A1: first-party Codex lead account; strongest for intent and confirmation.
- A2: official OpenAI account/status source.
- B: scout or relay with demonstrated discovery value; not authority.
- C: known rumor account; requires corroboration.
- D: broad discovery pool; candidate only.

## Event types

- `scheduled_reset`: future signal.
- `explicit_reset`: already confirmed.
- `weak_hint`: euphemism or ambiguous first-party clue.
- `community_rumor`: future community claim.
- `community_observation`: one account's local observation.
- `rate_limit_change`: quota/rule change, not necessarily a reset.

## Reset modes

- `hard_reset`: usage window is automatically restored for a broad scope.
- `banked_reset`: saved/reset credit that may require manual use.
- `unknown`: evidence is insufficient to distinguish.
- `not_applicable`: event is a rule/feature change rather than reset delivery.
